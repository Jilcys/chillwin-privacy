# Privacy Policy for Chillwin

**Last updated: 6 November 2026**

Chillwin is a single-player, offline game developed by Jilcys. This policy
explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

## Summary

**Chillwin does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** The game
has no network functionality, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising,
and no third-party SDKs. It requests no Android permissions that touch your
data or your device. The only permission in the Play build,
`com.android.vending.CHECK_LICENSE`, is added by the game engine's Android
build tools; it lets Google Play confirm the copy is licensed and reads nothing
from your device.

## Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal or device
information. There is no server to send it to — the app never connects to
the internet.

## Information stored on your device

Chillwin saves your game progress to a single file in the app's private
storage area on your device:

- Your ladder rating, rank, and win/loss record
- Your current run (upgrades, relics, in-progress battle)
- Unlocked achievements and run history
- Your settings (audio volume, battle speed, vibration, reduce motion)

This file never leaves your device unless you choose to share it. It contains
no personal information — no name, email, contacts, location, advertising
identifier, or device identifier. Android's automatic cloud backup is
disabled for this app, so the file is not copied to any Google service.

Uninstalling Chillwin deletes this file and all progress with it. You can
also erase it at any time from **Settings → Reset Progress** inside the game.

## Transfer codes

Chillwin can show you a "transfer code" — a block of text representing your
own game progress — so you can move a save between your own devices. The app
only displays this text. It does not upload or transmit it. If you copy a
transfer code somewhere (a message to yourself, a note), you are sharing it
yourself, and only the game progress described above is inside it.

## Children's privacy

Chillwin does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13. No
data collection means nothing to disclose, sell, or delete on request.

## Third parties

Chillwin contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or
social SDKs. The game is built with the Godot Engine, which is compiled into
the app and does not transmit data.

If you obtained Chillwin from the Google Play Store, Google may collect
information about the download and your device as part of operating the
store. That processing is governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), not this one,
and happens outside the app.

Chillwin is sold through Google Play for a one-time price. Google processes
that payment; the developer never receives your name, card, or billing
details, only aggregated sales figures. The game itself contains no in-app
purchases and no advertising.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Chillwin ever collects or transmits data, this policy
will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be
described here.
